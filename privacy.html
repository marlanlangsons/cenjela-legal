Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

Cenjela is a business management application designed to help small businesses record and understand information such as sales, expenses, inventory, customers, suppliers, debts, payments and business performance.

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide directly when you create an account or use Cenjela, including your name, email address, business details, product information, sales records, expenses, customer information, supplier information, payment records and other business data you choose to enter.

We may also collect technical information such as device type, operating system, app version, diagnostic information and usage information needed to operate, secure and improve the service.

How we use information

We use information to provide Cenjela's features, store and display your business records, calculate business summaries and reports, manage your account and subscription, improve reliability and security, and support future smart or AI-powered business features.

AI-powered features

When Cenjela offers AI-powered features, information you submit to those features may be processed by service providers used to generate the requested result. Cenjela is designed to show structured information for confirmation before important records are saved.

Payments and subscriptions

Mobile subscription payments may be processed by Apple or Google. Cenjela may use RevenueCat to manage subscription status and entitlements. Cenjela does not receive your full payment card details from the app stores.

Third-party services

Cenjela may use third-party providers for authentication, databases, hosting, subscription management, analytics, AI processing and related technical services. These providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services.

Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse or disclosure. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and maintain security. Users may request deletion of their account and associated data, subject to legal or operational retention requirements.

Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete personal information associated with your account.

Children

Cenjela is intended for business use and is not designed for children under the minimum age required to enter into digital service agreements in their jurisdiction.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Cenjela evolves. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Cenjela team using the official support contact provided in the application or store listing.
